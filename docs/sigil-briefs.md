# Sigil design briefs — original MysteryD glyphs

Operator directive (2026-11-15): Symbolikon's drawn artwork is NOT public domain
(paid pack: $4 private / $9 commercial per symbol). We draw our OWN glyphs.
Names and literary descriptions are used only as the creative basis — the
entities originate in public-domain fiction (Lovecraft, Chambers, Clark Ashton
Smith, Robert E. Howard, Frank Belknap Long). No Symbolikon artwork is embedded,
traced, or shipped.

Each brief below is the prompt basis for one glyph. Rendered as original
stroke-path line art in `atmosphere.js` (canvas, ink palette, 1.6px stroke),
so they sit in the case-file world as hand-drawn occult marginalia. If a
raster/AI render is ever wanted, the brief doubles as the generation prompt.

Style contract for every glyph:
- Single continuous-feeling line drawing, geometric/occult register.
- Symmetry or near-symmetry; readable at 36–68px on paper.
- No fills, no gradients; stroke only, round caps.
- Each must be visually distinct in silhouette from the other 15.

## The 16 briefs

1. **Elder Sign** — protective ward. A five-pointed star with distorted,
   flaming edges; Lovecraft himself drew it as a six-pointed branch. Basis:
   star with a central eye-like flame.
2. **Yig** — Father of Serpents, Great Old One, easy to anger when his snakes
   are harmed ("The Curse of Yig"). Basis: coiled serpent spiral with a
   raised head and forked tongue.
3. **The Yellow Sign** — glyph of Hastur and Carcosa (Chambers, "The King in
   Yellow"); whoever sees it is doomed. Basis: three curling tentacle-like
   strokes radiating from a broken triskelion center.
4. **Cthulhu** — the sleeping priest, octopus head, dragon wings, mountainous
   bulk. Basis: downward curling tentacle cluster beneath a winged arch.
5. **Dagon** — amphibious lord of the deep, consort of Mother Hydra,
   worshipped at Innsmouth. Basis: fish-scale arc over a trident of waves.
6. **Nyarlathotep** — the Crawling Chaos, tall swarthy pharaoh who walks the
   earth in human guise. Basis: tall fluted crown/obelisk over a striding
   angular figure.
7. **Hastur** — the Unspeakable, half-brother of Cthulhu, god of shepherds in
   later tales. Basis: tattered pall/hood shape over a shepherd's crook.
8. **Yog-Sothoth** — the All-in-One, the gate and the key, congeries of
   iridescent globes outside time. Basis: cluster of intersecting circles
   with a keyhole axis.
9. **Azathoth** — the blind idiot god at the center of infinity, seething
   nuclear chaos, piped to by flutes. Basis: broken concentric rings around
   a void center, radial chaos ticks.
10. **Atlach-Nacha** — spider god with a human face, spinning the bridge
    between the waking world and the Dreamlands; when the web is complete,
    the world ends. Basis: orb-web with a small face at the hub.
11. **Tsathoggua** — the Sleeper of N'kai, amorphous toad-like god, lazy and
    hungry when woken. Basis: squat toad silhouette, closed eyes, heavy
    folded limbs.
12. **Ghatanothoa** — so hideous that seeing it petrifies the viewer forever,
    brain preserved and aware; trapped under Mount Yaddith-Gho. Basis:
    medusa-like mass of writhing strokes inside a mountain triangle.
13. **Nodens** — the Hunter, Lord of the Great Abyss, Elder God who rides a
    chariot drawn from a huge seashell and hunts the Old Ones. Basis:
    hunting horn over a shell-spiral chariot wheel.
14. **Gloon** — Corrupter of Flesh, slug-like horror disguised as a beautiful
    Dionysian statue ("The Temple"). Basis: classical column capital with a
    slug trail coiling beneath.
15. **Rhan-Tegoth** — insectoid amphibious god-being, barrel trunk, six
    clawed appendages, three small eyes; first of the Old Ones to wake.
    Basis: barrel body, three eyes in a row, six pincer strokes.
16. **Y'golonac** — headless god of depravity, a mouth in each palm, summoned
    by reading his name in the Revelations of Gla'aki. Basis: headless
    torso outline with an open mouth in each outstretched hand.
