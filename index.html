03

The nine steps

03

Nine steps, in order, on every case. Even when the answer looks obvious, we run the step that would confirm it.

  1. 1

    Discover

    We learn what the product is meant to do and where it tends to fail.

  2. 2

    Scope

    We agree on exactly what will be examined and what will not.

  3. 3

    Plan

    We write the test plan before touching the product.

  4. 4

    Prepare

    We set up accounts, data and environments so the tests are repeatable.

  5. 5

    Execute

    We run the plan and try to break each flow on purpose.

  6. 6

    Report

    We write up every finding in plain English with steps to reproduce.

  7. 7

    Retest

    We check each fix against the original reproduction.

  8. 8

    Regression

    We sweep the surrounding flows to confirm the fix broke nothing else.

  9. 9

    Close

    We close the case only when every finding is resolved or accepted by you.

FAQ

04
How fast can you start?

Intake is read within 24 hours. A scoped engagement usually starts within a week; release-verification passes can be slotted faster when the date is fixed.

Do you work under NDA?

Yes. We sign your NDA or provide ours before any build or credential changes hands. Confidentiality is the default, not an add-on.

What do you need from us?

A build or staging URL, test accounts, and a paragraph on what the product is supposed to do. The intake form is the whole starting requirement.

What if you find nothing?

You get a case file saying exactly what was examined and what passed, with the coverage map. A clean result is a result; we never invent findings to justify the invoice.

How is pricing set?

Fixed scope, fixed price, agreed before work starts. The budget field on the form helps us scope honestly; if the ask does not fit the budget we say so at intake.
